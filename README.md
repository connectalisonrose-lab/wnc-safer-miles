# Buncombe Safer Miles

A community map of parks, greenways and trails in Buncombe County, NC, scored on what makes a place feel safer for women running or walking alone: lighting, foot traffic, sightlines, cell signal, staff presence and visible parking. Women rate places anonymously through a short form, and their ratings blend into each place's score.

- `index.html` is the whole site.
- `ratings.json` holds aggregated ratings (score, time of day, tags) and nothing else. It is refreshed automatically each morning from the rating form's responses.

The map rates places, never people. It never shows who rated, when anyone goes, or anyone's location.
